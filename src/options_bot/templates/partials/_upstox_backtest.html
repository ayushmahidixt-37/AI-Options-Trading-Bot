Historical backtest (Upstox)

Read-only historical data from Upstox, used only for offline backtesting. Upstox can never place an order and is not a second trading broker. Disabled unless UPSTOX_BACKTEST_ENABLED=true and UPSTOX_ACCESS_TOKEN is configured.
